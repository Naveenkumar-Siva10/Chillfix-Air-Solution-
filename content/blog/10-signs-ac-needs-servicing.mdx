Chennai's climate is no joke. With temperatures regularly exceeding **40°C** in summer and near-constant humidity, your air conditioner works overtime — and when it starts failing, you'll know it.

The problem? Most homeowners ignore early warning signs until the AC breaks down completely. That's when a minor maintenance visit turns into an expensive compressor repair.

Here are the **10 signs your AC needs immediate servicing** — catch them early and save money.

## 1. Warm or Weak Airflow

This is the most obvious sign. If your AC is running but the air coming out is warm or barely cool, something is wrong. The most common causes are:

- **Low refrigerant gas** — the gas has leaked and needs refilling
- **Dirty evaporator coils** — dust buildup reduces heat absorption
- **Compressor issues** — the heart of your AC may be struggling

**What to do:** Call a technician immediately. Low gas can damage the compressor if ignored.

## 2. Ice or Frost on the Indoor Unit

Seeing ice forming on your AC's indoor unit or copper pipes? That's a red flag. It usually means:

- **Refrigerant leak** — low gas causes coils to freeze
- **Blocked airflow** — dirty filters or closed vents
- **Fan motor failure** — poor air circulation over coils

Turn off the AC immediately and call for service. Running a frozen AC can destroy the compressor.

## 3. Water Leaking Inside

A little condensation is normal. But if you see water **dripping from your indoor unit onto your floor or walls**, that's a problem:

- **Clogged drain pipe** — most common cause, easily fixed
- **Frozen and thawing coils** — see sign #2
- **Cracked condensate pan** — pan needs replacement

Water damage to walls and ceilings can be expensive. Get it fixed quickly.

## 4. Strange Noises

Your AC should be quiet. Unusual noises indicate mechanical problems:

| Sound | Possible Cause |
|---|---|
| Banging or clunking | Loose or broken component inside |
| Hissing | Refrigerant leak |
| Clicking repeatedly | Electrical or thermostat problem |
| Grinding | Motor bearing failure |
| Squealing | Belt issue (older units) |

**Never ignore strange sounds.** What starts as a minor rattle often leads to major repairs.

## 5. Foul or Musty Odors

If your AC smells musty when it starts, mold or bacteria is growing inside the unit. In Chennai's humid climate, this is very common. A deep cleaning with anti-bacterial treatment is the solution.

A burning smell is more serious — it indicates **overheating components or electrical issues**. Turn off the AC immediately and call us.

## 6. Frequent On/Off Cycling (Short Cycling)

Does your AC turn on for a few minutes, shut off, and repeat? This "short cycling" wastes electricity and damages the compressor. Causes include:

- Oversized AC for the room
- Refrigerant leak
- Thermostat malfunction
- Frozen evaporator coil

## 7. High Electricity Bills

If your electricity bill has spiked without explanation, your AC is likely running inefficiently. A dirty AC works 20–30% harder to cool the same space, burning more electricity. Regular servicing typically reduces electricity consumption significantly.

## 8. Thermostat Problems

If the AC doesn't respond to temperature changes or runs constantly regardless of your settings, the thermostat may be faulty. Modern ACs can also suffer from PCB (circuit board) issues that mimic thermostat problems.

## 9. Visible Mold or Dust Around Vents

Black spots or dust buildup around your AC vents means the filters are overloaded and mold may be circulating through your air. This is a health risk — especially for children, elderly, and people with allergies.

## 10. AC is Over 2 Years Old Without Servicing

This one isn't a malfunction — it's prevention. If your AC hasn't been serviced in over 2 years, **it needs attention regardless of how it seems to be running**. Hidden issues build up silently until they cause a complete breakdown.
