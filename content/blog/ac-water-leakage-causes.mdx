Water leaking from an indoor Split AC unit onto your bedroom floor or office desk is a very common issue in Chennai. 

---

### Top 5 Causes of AC Water Leakage

1. **Clogged Drain Pipe:** Dust and jelly-like algae build up in the condensate line, forcing water to spill over the indoor drain pan.
2. **Frozen Evaporator Coils:** Low gas or choked filters cause ice to form on coils. When the ice melts rapidly, it overflows the tray.
3. **Cracked Condensate Pan:** Aging plastic pans can develop hairline cracks.
4. **Improper Unit Slope:** If the Split AC wall bracket wasn't leveled with a slight tilt towards the drain hole, water cannot flow out.
5. **Damaged Pipe Insulation:** Condensation forms on uninsulated copper pipes, causing continuous water drops.
