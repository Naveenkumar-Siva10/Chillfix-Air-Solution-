Refrigerant gas is essential for your AC to cool air. Unlike engine oil, AC gas does not burn up during normal operation — if gas is low, there is a leak!

---

### 5 Signs Your AC Has a Gas Leak

1. **AC Blows Warm Air:** The fan runs normally, but output air temperature stays above 26°C.
2. **Ice Formation on Outdoor Copper Pipe:** Thin discharge pipe builds up white frost or ice layer.
3. **Hissing or Bubbling Sound:** Caused by pressurized refrigerant escaping tiny pinholes.
4. **Higher Electricity Bills:** Compressor runs non-stop attempting to reach target room temperature.
5. **Oily Residue Near Copper Flare Joints:** Oil circulates with gas; dark oily spots signal a leak.
