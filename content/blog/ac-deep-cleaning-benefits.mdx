Many homeowners wonder whether to opt for a general service or a high-pressure jet-wash deep cleaning. 

---

### What is the Difference?

- **General Servicing:** Filters are washed, front panel wiped, and gas pressure checked.
- **Foam Jet-Wash Deep Cleaning:** A waterproof catch-bag is mounted around the indoor unit. Chemical foam is sprayed deep into aluminum cooling fins and blower wheels, followed by high-pressure water jet flushing. Outdoor unit coils are also jet-washed.

---

### Key Benefits of AC Deep Cleaning

1. **Restores 30% Lost Cooling Efficiency:** Removes compressed dust cakes choking internal coils.
2. **Eliminates Mold & Foul Bacteria:** Chemical foam destroys black mold spores living inside condensate trays.
3. **Cuts Electricity Bills:** Clean coils allow heat transfer without forcing compressor overload.
4. **Prevents Water Dripping:** Flushes out heavy jelly sludge in drain pipes.
