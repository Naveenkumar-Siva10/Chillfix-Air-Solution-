Chennai's tropical climate features high temperatures and heavy humidity for over 9 months of the year. This heavy usage places continuous strain on residential and office air conditioners.

---

### Recommended Servicing Frequency in Chennai

- **Mesh Filter Cleaning:** Every 4 to 6 weeks (Can be done by homeowner).
- **General Wet Servicing:** Twice a year (Once before peak summer in February/March, once post-monsoon in October/November).
- **Foam Jet-Wash Deep Cleaning:** Once a year for heavy-use home ACs, twice a year for commercial/office units.

---

### Why Servicing Frequency Matters in Coastal Chennai

Coastal air contains micro-salt particles that cause corrosion on copper coils. Regular servicing with protective coatings prevents coil pinhole leaks.
