Customer support often receives calls asking: *"Do I need AC servicing or AC repair?"* Understanding the difference saves time and helps you get the exact resolution for your AC problem.

---

### Comparison: AC Service vs AC Repair

| Parameter | AC Service (Preventive) | AC Repair (Breakdown) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Goal** | Cleaning, maintenance & efficiency | Fixing broken components |
| **AC Condition** | AC is working, but cooling is slow | AC is not turning on, leaking, or making noise |
| **Typical Tasks** | Jet wash coils, clean filters, top-up gas | Replace PCB, fix gas leak, swap capacitor/compressor |
| **Starting Cost** | ₹249 – ₹449 | ₹349 + spare parts |

If your AC is operating normally but needs pre-summer preparation, book [AC Service in Chennai](/services/ac-service-chennai). If your unit has stopped cooling, book [AC Repair in Chennai](/services/ac-repair-chennai).
